Have you been to the Mystery Park ?

The Mystery Park

I'm kind of obsessed with this amusement park.

I went there three times when I was a kid. I always went with my father, and thinking back on it, I'm not sure it was really responsible of him to take his children to a supposedly scientific place that was actually promoting conspiracy theories. BUT, I LOVED IT, I loved this place. It had a "La soirée de l'étrange" vibe. It looked like something out of a movie like Close Encounters of the Third Kind, Contact or ZATHURA.

And what drives me crazy is that NOBODY seems to know about this place. It has become a recurring question when I meet someone: "Have you been to the mystery park ?" and I never met someone who even heard about it. If you went there, or if you just knew about it, please tell me, write me at mail@robiiiiiiiiiiiin.net and tell me.

The Place

It is (was) located in Interlaken, in Switzerland. It still exists today, but it has been renamed and transformed into a kind of gigantic kindergarten and the attractions have been closed.

The building still bears witness to the cultist / conspiracy / bizarre / alien vibe it had. The building is shaped like a round space station with height branches going from its center with a circle going through the branches. At the center stands a tower overlooked by a big globe, a kind of control tower. At the ends of each branches stands a themed building that all looks different. The most noticeable are the egyptian pyramid and the mayan temple.

The Pavilions / Attractions

The main attractions were located in the pavilions at the end of each branches. Each attraction had a specific theme, always related to theories about ancient civilisations. It was often about the ancient astronauts theory.

The attractions were more like what 4DX is today than rollercoasters. It has a setup where you were sometimes sitting, sometimes standing, there were some physical props and a lot of special effects using lights, lasers, holograms amd smoke. Then it was a kind of documentary, narated by a voice-over, that was explaining the mystery and the theories surrounding it. And at some point, there were always aliens involved.

Pavilion 1 - Vimana

Vimanas: Space Shuttles of Ancient India

Ancient Indian writings tell of flying "vimanas". Sanskrit texts documented not only general stories of ancient flying, but also the descriptions of flying machines itself. At least 20 passages in the Rigveda refer to flying machines - three floors high, triangular and three-wheeled with enough room to carry several passengers.

"A machine that moves by its own power like a bird, whether on land, in water or in the air, is called a vimana. You, the gods, know the secret of building flying machines."

The fact is that many of the ancient traditions contain technical and scientific knowledge that has only been discovered in our time with the help of modern methods and instruments. What were the sources of knowledge in ancient India? Where did the documented astronomical knowledge, the calculations of planetary sizes and orbits, and the mysterious fact that even information about star trails from the universe has been handed down come from?

Who taught the Indians mathematics and physics - including facts on the age of the universe and the speed of light?

Pavilion 5 - Contact

Whenever a civilization comes into contact with visitors and their sophisticated technologies, which the less advanced civilization does not understand, a situation is created known as the "God effect". Another term for it is "cargo cult". This is how the visitors came to be worshipped as "Gods" or other supernatural beings by such tribes and peoples.

[...]

The Kayapo tribe in Brazil worship a being called Bep-Kororoti - "astronaut-like" god today symbolized by a straw man - and recite its messages - more proof of a living mythology that, to this day, relates information from a dark past.



In Colombia small "plane-like" objects of pure gold have been found. The way the wings of these objects had been arranged and their upright tail fin prompted the Aeronautical Institute in New York to start wind-tunnel experiments. Dr. Arthur Poyslee came to a positive conclusion as to the objects' ability to fly. One of these gold models has been reconstructed in a true-to-life fashion, with a wingspan of 87 cm. On August 26, 1996, this model took to its first remotecontrolled solo flight, which lasted several minutes - its performance was convincing and successful in all flight attitudes. An achievement made possible by German engineers. Designer: Peter Belting. Concept by: Dr. Algund Eenboom and Conrad Lübbers.

[...]

Would it be possible that even ancient Biblical reports might serve as historical sources? The prophet Ezekiel witnessed a "celestial vehicle" and described its many different technical details. As a matter of fact, Ezekiel himself traveled in it and was taken to a strange temple like building. When reconstructing this account, it becomes apparent that both the description of the vehicle and of the hangar-type building could have been based on facts.

Aside about antique airplanes

In the "Contact" brochure, there is a paragraph about the Incan Golden Airplanes.

Some golden miniature "airplanes" have been found in Colombia. Some say that these are miniature representations of ancient aircraft while some says these are just representations of birds and fish.

The Mystery Park brochure mentions Arthur Poyslee, Peter Belting, Algund Eenboom and Conrad Lübbers. Arthur Poyslee was the first to claim, in the 70's, that these artifacts were actually able to fly. 20 years later, the three other guys built exact replicas increased in size by a factor of 16 and equipped them with engines and radio control systems. In 1997 they performed a flight test at the Ancient Astronauts Society World Conference and the replicas were actually able to lift off, fly and perform maneuvers. The obvious conclusion was that the Inca objects were proven to be actual replicas of 100% functional aircrafts.

This website (kimcampion.com) writes about this and I personnaly find this website really cool in a goofy conspiracy theorist way.

Peter Belting, Algund Eenboom and another guy even wrote a book about this subject: "Die Flugzeuge der Pharaonen: antike Flugtechniken in drei Kontinenten" (The Pharaohs' Aircraft: Ancient Flight Techniques on Three Continents). The description is lovely: " [...] Ancient engineers did not use primitive flying machines, but rather highly efficient, state-of-the-art aircraft similar to those used today in space shuttles. [...] Small gliders were already flying in the realm of the pharaohs over 2300 years ago. [...] A functioning miniature jet engine shows that any village blacksmith in ancient Egypt could have manufactured engines. [...] This book presents the evidence of their functionality

Erich-von-Daniken.md

All this has been created by Erich von Däniken.

Däniken is born in 1935 in Switzerland and is the author of multiple books but he became known mainly because of "The Chariots of the Gods". A book theorising that humanity has been visited by extraterrestrials who brought us knowledges. Apparently, we can find evidence of this in numerous archeological sites. This book greatly contributed to popularizing the Ancient Astronauts theory.

The Ancient Astronauts theory is a CENTRAL part of the mystery park. Almost everything is related to this theory.

The theories of Däniken go further. According to him, humans were created by aliens.

In the early 70's, some people influenced by The Chariots of the Gods founded "The Ancient Astronaut Society". Today, the society has become the "Archaeology, Astronautics and SETI Research Association (A.A.S. R.A.)" and its congress was held at the Mystery Park.
