One of the greatest challenges facing research today is the question of whether extraterrestrial life exists. SETI (Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence) is the name of the international scientific research project that is tackling this challenge. Are there any traces that indicate visits by extraterrestrials to our Milky Way, or even to Earth?

Scientists agree: we are not alone in the cosmos. The probability is high that we will make contact. And even that “others” could have visited our solar system and Earth several times already. The search for clues is on!
