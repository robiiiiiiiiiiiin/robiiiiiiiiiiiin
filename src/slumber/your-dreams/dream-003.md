I woke up sad, gray, uncomfortable because I dreamt you died. I received a letter from your mother explaining that you went on a humanitarian mission and were killed. I was invited to come at your place, where a kind of ceremony was held. I went there with you. Like... another you. I hadn't the courage to tell you that you were dead. I waited until we were at the front of the door. You were excited to see her again, but I was obliged to tell you the truth. You were way more sad than I thought you would be. You fell on the floor sobbing, then I woke up.
