Däniken is born in 1935 in Switzerland and is the author of multiple books but he became known mainly because of "The Chariots of the Gods". A book theorising that humanity has been visited by extraterrestrials who brought us knowledges. Apparently, we can find evidence of this in numerous archeological sites. This book greatly contributed to popularizing the <strong>Ancient Astronauts theory</strong>.

[The Ancient Astronauts theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_astronauts) is a CENTRAL part of the mystery park. Almost everything is related to this theory.

The theories of Däniken go further. According to him, humans were created by aliens.

In the early 70's, some people influenced by The Chariots of the Gods founded "The Ancient Astronaut Society". Today, the society has become the "Archaeology, Astronautics and SETI Research Association ([A.A.S. R.A.](https://www.legendarytimes.com/))" and its congress was held at the Mystery Park.
