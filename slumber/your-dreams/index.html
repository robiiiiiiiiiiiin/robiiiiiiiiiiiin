Lula

I dreamt about my childhood bestfriend A. . We were both in our late teens, supervising a groupe of younger teenagers playing in a very friendly, fresh and beautifully green forest. A warm, gentle sun was up, as were the genuine laughters of our whole team. The following night, I dreamt about her again. I don't remember what happened in that dream, but the next day I told another friend about it. She advised me to contact A. , which I did not. Two nights later, I dreamt about A. again. Strangely enough, I dreamt I told her about the dreams in which we met, as well as the fact I told a friend about them. As I woke up, I knew I had to message her. We are now planning an afternoon together.

We rarely see each other anymore. The last time we did was on the 19th of July 2023. It was nice to get to spend some quality time with her again. I wonder how she is doing. If you see this, I am eternally glad we met, A. ♡ (I'll let her know next time we meet.)
